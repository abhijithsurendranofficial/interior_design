Our story

Design is personal.
It should feel that way.

Live Space began with a simple belief: a well-designed room should make everyday life feel a little easier, a little more beautiful and a lot more like you.

Based in Kottayam, we work with homeowners and businesses to turn ideas into welcoming, well-considered interiors. We ask good questions, pay attention to the small things and make decisions with the whole space in mind.

From a single room to a complete home, our team brings creative thinking and steady project care to every brief.
